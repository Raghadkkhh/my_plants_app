# Planto 🌱

Planto is an iOS app that helps you take care of your plants. Add your plants in one place and get a reminder when it's time to water them.

# Main Features

- Add a plant with its name, room, light, watering days, and water amount
- See all your plants in one list with the room, light, and water amount for each
- Mark a plant as watered and track your daily progress with a progress bar
- Edit or delete a plant, or swipe a plant to delete it
- Get a local notification when it's time to water each plant
- A friendly empty state, a welcome screen for first-time users, and an "All Done" screen when every plant is watered
- Dark mode and Liquid Glass buttons

# Technologies Used

- Swift
- SwiftUI
- SwiftData
- MVVM architecture
- UserNotifications (local notifications)
- Liquid Glass (iOS 26)
